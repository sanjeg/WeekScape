About

WeekScape is a planner for iPhone and iPad built around the week as the fundamental unit of time. Drop plans into whichever week fits, pin a day only when it matters, and drag things around as life shifts. Your plans sync privately through your own iCloud account.

Frequently asked questions

How does iCloud sync work?

If your device is signed into iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled, plans sync automatically across your devices. You can check the sync status in the app under Settings → Storage. Without iCloud, plans are stored on your device only.

How do I move a plan to another week?

Press and hold a plan, then drag it onto another week card. You can also open the plan and pick a different week in the editor.

How do I delete a plan?

Swipe the plan to the left and tap the delete button, or swipe all the way across to delete in one motion. You can also delete from the plan editor.

Can I start my week on Sunday?

Yes — in the app, open Settings and switch “Start week on” between Sunday and Monday.
